My opinion on AI

I believe Generative AI is destructive for a terrifying number of reasons, both for humans and everything that makes us human, and the planet. Many references on this are given on Jean-Baptiste Cadart's essay on AI. My opinion on it in mathematics is less important, as mathematicians are just a pawn in the big capitalistic game of AI companies. However, I feel the need to address it separately since the situation on arXiv (and other AI slop websites) is only getting worse. My stance aligns perfectly with the one of Vladimir Lazić. For those reasons, I do not use Gen AI in any point of my thinking or creation process in mathematics (including proofreading, as it also requires thinking, no loophole there!). I also do not plan to read any papers written with Gen AI or collaborate with anyone who does so. By extension, I am a member of AHM .
